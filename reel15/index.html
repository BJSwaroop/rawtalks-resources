🎯 First 7 Days — Action Plan
Result vachina first week lo em cheyyaali — step by step.
1
Download Rank Card
Official website nunchi download cheyi. Screenshot kuda theesuko. Hall ticket number + DOB kavali login ki.
2
Check Previous Year Cutoffs
Careers360, CollegeDekho lo nee rank range ki em colleges vachaayi last year? List banao.
3
Make a College Shortlist (15-20)
Top 5 dream + 5 realistic + 5-10 safe options. Branch + college both consider cheyi.
4
Research Placements — Not Just Name
College website lo placement reports chudu. Average package, highest package, top recruiters — data basis lo decide cheyi.
5
Gather Documents
SSC memo, Inter memo, EAMCET hall ticket, rank card, caste/income certificates — ippudu nunche collect cheyi. Last moment rush vaddhu.
6
Talk to Current Students — Not Relatives
College lo actual students ni contact cheyi. Seniors ki DM cheyi. Relatives opinion kaadu — data kavali.
7
Avoid Agents & Paid Consultants
Counselling official process matrame follow cheyi. Seat guarantee cheppevaallani nammaku. Scam ekkuva undhi.
🏫 Rank-Wise Quick Guide
Nee rank range ki em options unnaayi — quick overview.
Rank 1 – 5,000 (Top Tier)
JNTUK UCE, AUCE, SVUCE, GVP, RVRJC lo CSE/IT/ECE. Government colleges lo priority iyyyi. Branch + college rendu dorukuthaay.
Rank 5,000 – 15,000 (Strong)
Government colleges lo ECE/EEE/Mech. Private lo CSE possible — VIT-AP, SRM-AP, KLU consider cheyi. ROI basis lo decide cheyi.
Rank 15,000 – 50,000 (Competitive)
Good private colleges lo CSE/IT vasthundi. GITAM, Vignan, BVRIT, CVR, Anurag, CBIT type colleges. Placement data chudu — name kaadu.
Rank 50,000 – 1,00,000 (Options Available)
Government fee reimbursement unte private lo join avvu. Smaller govt colleges kuda try cheyi. Skills build cheyi 1st year nunchi — college name kaadu, skills matter.
Rank 1,00,000+ (Don't Panic)
Options unnaayi: Spot admission, lateral entry (diploma), other state exams, or alternative paths (BSc, BCA, online degree). Oka door moosthe inkoka terusthundhi.
💡 Key Insight
Rank 50,000+ students: college name kaadu — nuvvu em chestaav college lo, adhi matter. Coding, projects, internships — ivi nee real placement weapons.

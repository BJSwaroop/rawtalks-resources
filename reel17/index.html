💡 Score Improve Tips

Last-minute preparation lo ee strategies follow cheyyi — 10-20 marks difference vasthundhi.

1. No Negative Marking = Attempt Everything
80 questions lo even 5 guesses correct ayina = 10 marks extra. Never leave blank!
2. Maths First = Maximum Impact
80/160 marks Maths lone. Maths lo 5 extra questions correct = rank lo 2000-3000 difference!
3. PYQ Pattern Follow Cheyyi
Last 5 years PYQs lo 30-40% questions repeat avthaayi with slight variations. Pattern recognition = easy marks.
4. Time Management — 2.25 min/question
180 min / 80 questions = 2.25 min each. First round lo easy vaati 1 min lo, tough vaati skip — second round lo attempt.
5. Chemistry Theory = Free Marks
Inorganic chemistry lo 5-6 questions pure theory (p-block, d-block). One night revision = guaranteed 10-12 marks.
Remember: IPE weightage ledu = nee entrance performance alone matters. Inter lo marks baaga lekapoyina, EAMCET lo baaga chesthe top rank vasthundhi. No excuse — just prepare smart.

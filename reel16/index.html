Fee Reimbursement: AP Government provides fee reimbursement for SC, ST, BC, EBC, Minority, and economically weaker students (family income < 2.5 LPA for BC, < 1 LPA for EBC). Convener Quota seats only. Apply through Jnanabhumi portal.
Convener Quota vs Management Quota
Convener (70%): EAMCET rank based. Lower fees. Counselling through APSCHE.
Management (30%): College direct admission. Higher fees. No rank needed — but EAMCET qualification required.
⚠️ Beware: Some colleges charge "development fees", "caution deposits" on top of tuition. Check total cost including hostel before committing. Get fee structure in writing!
Education Loans: SBI, Bank of Baroda, Canara Bank offer education loans for engineering at 8-10% interest. Collateral-free up to 7.5 LPA. Vidya Lakshmi portal (vidyalakshmi.co.in) lo apply cheyyi.
📋 Counselling Smart Tips

Counselling lo ee mistakes chesthey seat potthundhi. Jagrathaga chadvuu.

Mistake #1
College Brand Ki Priority Ivvadam
Big name college lo Mechanical theesko vaddu if you want IT career. Small autonomous college lo CSE > Big govt college lo Civil.
Mistake #2
Only Round 1 Lo Hope Pettadam
Round 2, 3, and spot rounds lo better colleges vasthaayi. Every round lo options improve avthaayi — don't settle too early.
Mistake #3
Location Ki Over-Importance Ivvadam
Home dhaggara college ante comfortable — but career ki best college better. 4 years adjust chesthey 40 years benefit.
Mistake #4
Placement Data Check Cheyyakapovadam
College website lo "100% placements" ante believe cheyyaku. LinkedIn lo alumni check cheyyi — vaallaki actual ga jobs vachaayi ante that college is worth it.
Mistake #5
Web Options Sariggaa Fill Cheyyakapovadam
Maximum options fill cheyyi (colleges + branches). Minimum 30-40 options pettali. Less options = less chances of better allotment.
Golden Rule: Branch > College > Location. IT career kaavaalante CSE/IT first priority. Government college lo non-CSE theesko vaddu if private lo CSE vasthundhi for same rank.

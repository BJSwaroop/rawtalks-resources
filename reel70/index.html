🤔 Asalu Negotiate Enduku Cheyali?

Chaala mandi ki idi telidu — starting salary lo chinna difference, career motham lo pedda difference avtundhi.

Compounding Effect
Prathi hike, prathi switch — nee current CTC meeda percentage ga calculate avtundhi. Start lo ₹1 LPA ekkuva unte, prathi future offer aa pedda number meeda base avtundhi.
Example: 4.5 LPA vs 5.5 LPA start. 3 job switches lo (avg 30% hike): 5 years tarvata difference = ₹8-12 lakhs+ total. Okka email tho.
"Offer Withdraw Chestharemo?" — Fear Reality
Idi biggest fear — kani polite negotiation valla offer withdraw avvadam extremely rare. Company already weeks spend chesindhi nee hiring meeda. Interviews, HR rounds, approvals — antha effort tarvata, polite ga oka question adigithe evaru offer cancel cheyaru.
Offer risk lo padedhi eppudu? Demanding ga matladithe, ultimatums isthey, fake competing offers cheppithe. Ee scripts lo avi levu.
Golden Rule: Nee leverage highest eppudu? Offer letter vachhaka, accept cheyaka mundhu. Aa window lo ne negotiate cheyali. Accept chesaka adigithe unprofessional ga untundhi.
Swaroop's Take: Adagadam free. Worst case "no" antaru — nuvvu gracefully accept chestav, image kuda padadhu. Best case ₹1-3 LPA extra. Risk zero, upside lakhs. Adugu!
📧 The Email Template

Idi exact email — copy chesi, yellow blanks fill chesi send cheyyi. Tone: grateful + confident + flexible.

Email Script
Subject: Regarding Compensation — [Role] Offer | [Your Name]

Dear [HR Name],

Thank you so much for offering me the [Role] position at [Company]. I'm genuinely excited about the opportunity and the team I'd be working with.

Before I formally accept, I wanted to ask if there is any flexibility in the compensation. Based on my skills in [Skill 1] and [Skill 2], and the current market range for this role, I was hoping for a package closer to ₹[X] LPA.

I want to be transparent — [Company] is my first choice, and if we can work this out, I'm ready to accept immediately.

Thank you for considering my request. Looking forward to hearing from you.

Warm regards,
[Your Name]
[Phone Number]
Enduku idi work avtundhi: Gratitude tho start (❤️ factor) → specific number with reason (serious candidate) → "ready to accept immediately" (HR ki easy close). Ee 3 elements unte reply positive ga untundhi.
⚠️ [X] value: Offered package meeda 10-20% ekkuva adugu. 4.5 LPA offer ki 5-5.4 adugu. Double adigithe conversation ne close avtundhi.
📞 Call Negotiation Script

HR call chesinappudu panic avvakunda — ee flow follow cheyyi. Prathi step ki exact words ichaam.

1
Thanks tho Start Cheyyi
"Thank you so much for the offer! I'm really excited about the role and the company."
Eppudu direct ga salary topic tho start cheyaku.
2
The Ask — Polite & Specific
"I had one small question — is there any flexibility in the compensation? Based on my skills and the market range for this role, I was hoping for something closer to ₹[X] LPA."
3
The Pause — Silence Is Power
Adigaka silent ga undu. Awkward anipinchina, matladaku. Nervous ga "sorry, it's okay if not possible..." ani fill cheyaku — adi nee ask ni weak chestundhi. HR ne respond avvani.
4
"Let me check" Antey
"That would be great, thank you! Should I wait for your confirmation before signing the offer letter?" — timeline fix cheyyi, follow-up right establish cheyyi.
5
Close — Positive Note
"Either way, I'm very excited to join the team. Thank you for considering!" — relationship damage avvakudadhu, answer emaina.
Pro Tip: Call lo verbal ga agree ayina — "Could you please share the revised details over email?" ani adugu. Email lo lekapothey adi official kaadhu.
💬 WhatsApp Version

India lo half hiring WhatsApp lo ne jarugutundhi. HR chat lo unte — ee short version use cheyyi.

WhatsApp Script
Hi [HR Name], thank you so much for the offer! I'm really excited about joining [Company]. 🙏

I had one small request — based on my skills and the current market range for this role, would it be possible to revisit the compensation to around ₹[X] LPA?

If we can work this out, I'm ready to accept right away. Thank you for considering!
WhatsApp Negotiation Rules
✅ Short ga undu — 3 chinna paragraphs max
✅ Polite emoji okati chalu (🙏) — ekkuva vaddu
✅ Reply ki 24-48 hrs time ivvu, spam cheyaku
✅ Final numbers eppudu email lo confirm cheskovali
❌ Voice notes pampaku — text professional
❌ "Bro/Sir please" tone vaddu — confident polite tone
Remember: WhatsApp casual medium ayina, nee tone professional ga undali. Chat lo ok cheppina tarvata — "Could you share the revised offer letter over email?" ani adagadam marchipoku.
🔒 HR "Package Fixed" Antey?

Freshers ki most common reply idi. Ikkada chala mandi give up chestharu — kani base move avvakapoyina, inka options unnaayi.

1
Acknowledge — Fight Cheyaku
"I completely understand that the package is standardized for freshers."
HR tho argue chesthe dead end. Accept chesi next step ki move avvu.
2
Pivot — Base Kakunda Vere Adugu
"In that case, would it be possible to consider a joining bonus, relocation assistance, or an early performance review at 6 months instead?"
Base fixed ayina — ivi flexible ga untaayi chala companies lo.
3
Writing Lo Teesko
Emi agree ayina — "Could you please include this in the offer letter / confirm over email?" Verbal promises join ayyaka gurthu undavu.
⚠️ 5 Mistakes — Ivi Cheyaku!
MISTAKE #1
Fake Competing Offer Cheppadam
"Vere company X isthundhi" ani lie cheppaku — HR proof adigithe trap. Real offer unte ne mention cheyyi.
MISTAKE #2
Ultimatum Ivvadam
"Increase cheyakapothey join avvanu" — idi negotiation kaadhu, threat. Flexibility chupinchu, demand kaadhu.
MISTAKE #3
"I Deserve" Tone
"Naaku ivvalsindhe" tone entitled ga untundhi. "Based on my skills and market range" — reason tho adugu.
MISTAKE #4
Accept Chesaka Negotiate Cheyadam
Offer letter sign chesaka salary adigithe unprofessional. Nee window: offer vachhaka, accept cheyaka mundhu.
MISTAKE #5
Friends Package Tho Compare Cheyadam
"Naa friend ki 6 ichharu" — HR ki idi irrelevant. Market data cite cheyyi (Glassdoor, AmbitionBox, LinkedIn), friends kaadhu.
💡 Bonus Tips — Cheat Sheet

Last ga — ee quick rules gurthu pettuko. Negotiation lo 90% success ee basics lo ne undi.

💰 Entha adagali? Offered CTC meeda 10-20% — defensible range
⏰ Eppudu? Offer letter vachhaka, accept cheyaka mundhu — only window
📊 Research: AmbitionBox, Glassdoor, LinkedIn lo role+city salary check cheyyi
🙏 First response: Ye offer ki aina mundhu thanks cheppu — tarvata negotiate
📧 Always email: Call/chat lo agree ayina, email confirmation adugu
😊 "No" vachhina: Gracefully accept cheyyi — join ayyaka 6 months lo prove chesko
Final Word from Swaroop: Nuvvu already offer earn chesav — interviews clear chesav, skills prove chesav. Ippudu nee worth adagadam lo thappu ledhu. Scripts ready ga unnaayi — use cheyyi, confident ga adugu. All the best! 🚀
